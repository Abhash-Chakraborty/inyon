Offline sync

How a list keeps working with no signal, and what happens when two people changed it before either was back online.

01What the phone keeps 02Merging edits

01What the phone keeps

Every list you opened in the last 30 days stays on the phone, with its items, ticks and notes. Edits made offline queue up in order and go out as soon as there's a connection.

02Merging edits

Each item merges on its own, so two people rarely clash. When they do, the rule is plain:

Both changedResult
The tickTicked wins. Unticking needs a connection.
The nameThe later edit wins; the other shows under Changed by Sam.
Delete and editThe item stays, with the edit, and a note says it was deleted elsewhere.
